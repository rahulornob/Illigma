# Changelog

All notable changes to Illigma are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versions will follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) once code ships.

## End goal

**Illigma v1.0 (October 2027):** an open-source, local-first interface design tool with Figma's editing model. Your files stay on your machine. No account, no cloud and no seat licence.

v1.0 is done when a working product designer can build and hand off a multi-screen app design with a component library, entirely offline, without opening Figma. That means:

- **Core Figma parity** for the daily-use 80%: frames, auto layout, constraints, components and variants, variables and modes, styles, text, vector editing, prototyping and export.
- **Local-first files:** an open, documented `.illigma` format on disk, with autosave, crash safety and full history.
- **Performance:** 10k-layer files open in under 2 s and pan at 60 fps.
- **A way in from Figma:** import from Figma (REST API and best-effort `.fig`), SVG and Penpot, with 95% fidelity on a 50-file test corpus.
- **Developer handoff:** an inspect mode with code snippets, token export and a local MCP server for AI agents.
- **An open ecosystem:** a plugin API close enough to Figma's that common plugins port with small changes.

After v1.0: a Tauri desktop app in v1.x, then optional multiplayer (CRDT over LAN or a self-hosted relay) in v2. The full scope, roadmap and gates are in [docs/PRD.md](docs/PRD.md).

**Where we are now:** visual design phase. Development starts once the core editor states are designed and approved in Figma (see [docs/design/README.md](docs/design/README.md)).

### Fixed (2026-10-19, QC round 7: tester fixes for the renderer and the web app)
- **Shadow spread on groups, text and frames without a fill is fast on CPU surfaces** (export and the CPU fallback canvas). Skia's morphology filter cost about a microsecond per pixel per pixel of spread there. Spread is now a chain of about log2(2 × spread) cheap passes (the maximum of the image and a shifted copy of itself, as `Lighten` on the alpha moved into opaque grey), matching Skia's result pixel for pixel; very large spreads and wide blurs run on a copy reduced by powers of two. Layers with effects are bounded by what they draw (recorded first), instead of covering the whole surface, and a shadow is knocked out only inside the layer's bounds. Inner shadows from pixels no longer go through an inverted, surface-sized image. On a 2000 × 2000 CPU surface: a group's shadow with spread 300 went from 72 s to about 30 ms per frame (300 ms at zoom 4, where it covers the whole surface); radius, spread and offset 1000 from about 180 s to 0.4 s; PNG export at 4x with spread 50 from 35.7 s to 0.6 s; PDF with spread 100 from 17.2 s to 0.3 s. On the CPU, layers with these effects are recorded for the zoom (rounded to a power of two) and recorded again when it changes. The GPU keeps Skia's own filters.
- **An 8000 × 8000 image fill no longer slows the canvas.** Images over 2048 × 2048 pixels are decoded off the main thread (`createImageBitmap`) and uploaded once as GPU textures, with half-size copies made on the GPU for when they are drawn small (they stand in for mipmaps, which Skia would rebuild for a texture on every frame). Panning over one: p50 100 ms → 16.7 ms, p95 133 ms → 16.8 ms; the longest task while opening the file: 366–813 ms → about 50 ms. The GPU resource cache budget is raised to 768 MB. New check in `perf.mjs`.
- **Text strokes are drawn**, over the fills: centred strokes as they are, inside and outside strokes at twice the weight cut to their side of the glyph outlines.
- **Images that couldn't be opened are reported.** Opening a file with a damaged or missing image, or a stray file in its package, shows a dismissable banner: "Some images couldn't be opened:" with the first three problems and how many more. New check in `canvas.mjs`.
- **An image added after a layer drew it as missing appears** (e.g. `Document.addImage` after the paint): the renderer listens for new images (`RenderSource.subscribeImages`) and re-records the layers that drew it as missing.
- `PageRenderer.layoutText` lines also give `ascent`, `descent` and `width`, so export can bound text exactly.
- **End-to-end runs can't hang.** A script whose step makes no progress for 120 s (`E2E_STEP_TIMEOUT`, scaled) fails with the step's name and a screenshot instead of waiting forever (Playwright's `page.evaluate` has no timeout of its own), and `run-all.mjs` stops a script that runs past 10 minutes (`E2E_SUITE_TIMEOUT`), browser included, and goes on with the rest. Closing the browser and the server can no longer hang a run either.
- Tests: 14 renderer tests (pixels against Skia's own filters, timing budgets, text strokes, layoutText, image levels, images added later), 4 web app tests, and e2e checks in `canvas.mjs` and `perf.mjs`.

### Fixed (2026-10-18, QC round 7: layers, resize and vectors)
- Layers panel: a selection made in the list keeps the row you're on in view; only selections made elsewhere scroll to the selection. Shift+↑, Shift-click upward and ⌘-click to deselect no longer scroll the list away, and Shift+Home from far down keeps keyboard focus in the list (↓ then moves in the list instead of nudging the canvas). Deleting the focused row moves focus to the next row (or the previous one), not to the page. Rows no longer claim `aria-expanded` while they can't collapse.
- `resizeMany` with a frame and layers inside its groups: constraints work from the document's values, so a layer's (or a group's) own box is no longer scaled a second time. A property test checks the batch against resizing one by one, parents first.
- Vector networks scale without rounding to a millionth (12 significant digits instead), so scaling far down and back keeps their shape; coordinates that would overflow keep the old network instead of making the whole resize fail.
- SVG paths: an open subpath counts as enclosing an area only when its area isn't zero (`M0 0 h10 h10` doesn't). With the even-odd rule and overlapping subpaths, the closed subpaths keep their region (holes stay open) and an open subpath that encloses an area goes unfilled; otherwise every run fills as if closed, as in SVG.
- Tests: 11 unit tests, plus e2e checks in `canvas.mjs` and `perf.mjs`.
### Fixed (2026-10-18, QC round 7: images and hit-testing)
- **Images in big frames appear.** An image inside a top-level frame with more than 256 children (recorded in chunks), or on that frame itself, never showed: the frame wasn't re-recorded once the image decoded. It is now.
- **The canvas redraws when an image decodes**, with no click or pointer move needed (the editor passes `onImageReady`). New e2e check in `canvas.mjs`.
- **No more lost images on save.** Saves never remove image files. A damaged, oversized or misnamed image file Illigma couldn't load is left as it is (its layers keep the hash), instead of being deleted by the next save. Unused image files are removed only when a file opens with no recovered edits pending, and only sound ones (`ReadReport.unusedImages`, `Backend.collectImages`).
- **Images survive undo and crashes.** Saves write every image the document holds, so deleting an image layer, saving, undoing and crashing keeps the image. The recovery journal copies each added image (`Document.subscribeImages`) before the change lines that use it, so an image added after the last save comes back with Keep. With recovered edits pending, unused images on disk are loaded too.
- **Image size in pixels is capped**: 16,384 pixels a side and 64 megapixels, read from the PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF header before decoding (`imageDimensions`, `imageSizeProblem`, `IMAGE_LIMITS.maxSide` and `maxPixels`). A 1.5 MB file of 20,000 × 20,000 pixels no longer makes the canvas allocate gigabytes: `addImage` refuses it, and opening leaves it out with a plain warning (and on disk).
- A save checks an image already on disk once per session (by hash) before skipping it, so a damaged copy of the same size is repaired.
- Reading a package retries when another tab removes a file between listing and reading it, instead of failing.
- The renderer frees decoded images no cached picture uses any more.
- **Hit-testing of aligned strokes**: on closed shapes, inside strokes are hit over their whole weight inside the outline, and outside strokes over their whole weight outside it (no phantom inner band). Rectangles, ellipses and frames can be picked on a centred or outside stroke past their box; a frame still clips its children to its box.
- The prototype importer warns when it keeps only a layer's first 100 effects. Opening a file hashes images with `crypto.subtle` (`readPackageWithReportAsync`, `sha256HexAsync`) instead of JavaScript on the main thread.
### Fixed (2026-10-19, export QC round 7: tester findings)
- **Text past its box** is no longer cut off. Export bounds use the renderer's line layout (`PageRenderer.layoutText`): the box, plus the last line's descent below it, and the glyphs above it when a tight line height lifts them. A 200 × 40 text box with three 20 px lines exports at 200 × 72 in PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF, as on the canvas. PDF group forms, effect images and the text image cover the whole text; SVG filter regions too. SVG without fonts measures the lines it writes. New: `textLinesBounds`, `TextBounds`, and an optional `text` argument to `exportScene` and `renderBounds`.
- **SVG shadow spread** keeps the shape: a shape's shadows with a spread are SVG shapes grown or shrunk from its outline (rectangles keep square corners, rounded corners and ellipses grow with the spread, as the renderer does; paths grow by a mitred stroke of twice the spread and shrink through a mask), then moved and blurred. An ellipse's inner shadow with spread 10 was a lens with square corners (`feMorphology` grows with a square). Inner shadows go over the fills and under the strokes, as on the canvas. Groups, text and shapes with a one-sided stroke still use `feMorphology`, with a warning that it is approximate.
- **PDF of several layers**: hidden ones are left out with a warning instead of failing the whole file; it fails only when every layer is hidden.
- **File names**: `uniqueFilename` makes a raw path safe first (direction overrides, illegal characters, `.` and `..`, device names), so no public path keeps U+202E or an unreserved COM¹. Device names with spaces before the extension ("CON .png") are reserved too; U+061C, line and paragraph separators, U+2066–206F and the byte order mark are dropped. The dropped characters are written as escapes in the source (they were invisible literals). Every public path (exportNode, exportNodes, planExportFilenames, exportPdf, uniqueFilename, sanitizeFilePart) is tested. On the main build at 33d9ef0, `exportFilename` and the batch already gave `RTL gnp.exe.png` and `_COM¹.png`.
- **Missing glyphs**: PNG, JPG and PDF exports warn, naming up to five characters, when text uses characters none of the loaded fonts has (they draw as empty boxes), checked with CanvasKit's `Typeface.getGlyphIDs`.
- Tests: 14 in `packages/export/src/qc7-tester.test.ts`.

### Fixed (2026-10-18, export QC round 7)
- **Phone photos** (JPEG Exif orientation) are no longer squashed or misplaced in SVG and PDF: the header reader turns the size for orientations 5 to 8, and the writers store the image as it is in the file (orientation tag cleared) and turn it themselves, matching CanvasKit and the PNG.
- **PDF JPEGs** (8-bit grey or RGB, baseline or progressive) are embedded as they are even when CanvasKit is loaded, instead of being re-encoded (a 282 KB photo made a 2.48 MB PDF). Other images are decoded only up to 16,384 px a side and 64 million pixels (checked from the header first), else left out with a warning.
- **Mitred corners and square caps** count in export bounds (a polygon's apex was cut by about half its stroke weight in every format, and so were SVG filter regions, PDF group boxes and effect images). The SVG mask for outside strokes reaches the miter limit too.
- **PDF effects budget**: layers with shadows or blurs are drawn at 2 px per pt (was 4) within 8 million pixels per file, at a lower resolution when they don't fit, and left out (with a warning) below 0.25 px per pt; image data is compressed at zlib level 6. Five 2,000 px layers with a 1,000 blur: 32.6 s to 1.5 s; 100 of them: over 10 minutes to 1.4 s (measured in Node). A layer with only background blur is no longer drawn as an image (the blur is left out with a warning).
- **SVG**: a filter, opacity or layer blend mode no longer shares an element with a stroke's clip or mask (the shadow was clipped) or with a paint's blend mode (the layer's was lost); they get a `<g>`. A NORMAL group whose children blend is `isolation: isolate`. Shapes cast shadows from their outline as opaque geometry, as the renderer does (a half-transparent fill made a half-strength shadow, and inner shadows covered the whole shape). Generated ids start with a fingerprint of the file and the node id, so two inlined exports don't clash. Image tiles too small to write are enlarged, with a warning.
- **PDF**: a NORMAL group whose children blend, and every layer group, is an isolated transparency group (`/I true`). Text and layers drawn as images keep their blend mode. The image tile cap (10,000) is per page, not per paint.
- **Text** is measured for bounds when any fill shows, not only the first.
- **Batches**: a suffix that isn't text fails only that export (`ExportError`, listed in `failures`), not the whole batch with a raw `TypeError`.
- **File names**: CONIN$, CONOUT$, COM¹–³ and LPT¹–³ are reserved too, and device names are checked after the suffix and the length cut ("CO" + "N" was `CON.png`); direction controls (U+200E, U+200F, U+202A–202E, U+2066–2069) and unpaired surrogates are dropped; duplicates compare without case or accent encoding (NFC); a file and a folder never get the same path; long names are cut between whole graphemes.
- Tests: 21 in `packages/export/src/qc7.test.ts`.

### Added (2026-10-18, paints and effects in export)
- `@illigma/export` writes gradients, images, blend modes, effects and stroke alignment. PNG and JPG get them from the renderer, with image bytes from the document (`ExportSource.image`). Export bounds include drop shadows and layer blur.
- SVG: linear and radial gradients in the layer's space; one embedded image per file, used through patterns; `mix-blend-mode`; one `<filter>` per layer for drop shadows, inner shadows and layer blur; inside and outside strokes clipped or masked.
- PDF: shading patterns, image XObjects (decoded by CanvasKit, or JPEG as it is), `/BM` blend modes and clipped inside and outside strokes. Layers with shadows or blurs are drawn into an image by the renderer.
- Every approximation or omission is a warning in the export result: angular and diamond gradients written as radial, LINEAR_BURN, background blur in SVG, PDF gradient opacity, image strokes in PDF, missing images, and effects without CanvasKit.
- The prototype importer brings effects over: drop and inner shadows (offsets turned into the layer's space), layer and background blur.
- Tests: 14 export tests and 1 importer test.

### Added (2026-10-18, gradients, images, effects and blend modes)
- **Format** (`@illigma/format`, format 0, every new field optional, Figma Plugin API names). Paints are now `SOLID`, `GRADIENT_LINEAR`, `GRADIENT_RADIAL`, `GRADIENT_ANGULAR`, `GRADIENT_DIAMOND` (`gradientStops`, `gradientTransform` as a 2 × 3 matrix from the node's normalised box to gradient space, as in Figma) and `IMAGE` (`imageHash`, `scaleMode` FILL, FIT, CROP or TILE, `imageTransform` for CROP, `scalingFactor` for TILE). Every paint can have `opacity` and `blendMode`. Nodes can have `effects` (`DROP_SHADOW` and `INNER_SHADOW` with `color`, `offset`, `radius`, `spread`, `blendMode`, plus `showShadowBehindNode` on drop shadows; `LAYER_BLUR` and `BACKGROUND_BLUR` with `radius`), a `blendMode` (all 19 of Figma's, `PASS_THROUGH` included) and a `strokeAlign` (`CENTER`, `INSIDE` or `OUTSIDE`). Files without these fields read and save byte for byte as before. An absent `strokeAlign` means `CENTER`, because format 0 drew every stroke centred; Figma's default for new shapes is `INSIDE`, so the editor should set it explicitly.
- **Images in the package**: `images/<sha-256>.<ext>` (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, at most 32 MB each), with `imageHash` the SHA-256 of the bytes. `IlligmaFile.images` holds them in memory. `writePackage` writes only images that some paint uses, and refuses unknown formats, oversize files and bytes that don't match their hash. `readPackageWithReport` opens the file even when an image is damaged, unknown or oversize: it leaves that image out and adds a warning. It also warns about missing images and ignores unused or unrecognised files under `images/`, with a warning each. `FileTree` values are now `string | Uint8Array` (images are bytes, all other files text). Zips store images uncompressed and return them as bytes. New helpers: `sha256Hex` (synchronous), `hashImage`, `checkImage`, `sniffImageFormat`, `isBinaryPackagePath`, `referencedImageHashes` and `fileText`. Validation refuses unknown keys in paints and effects, and caps paints at 1,000, gradient stops at 1,000, effects at 100, and radius, spread and offset at 1,000.
- `@illigma/core`: `Document.addImage(bytes)` returns the hash, and `Document.image(hash)` reads it back. Images are content-addressed, live outside undo history, and go through `toFile` and `fromFile`.
- `@illigma/host-web`: backends read and write images as bytes, including the staged copies of a save that was cut short. An image already on disk is not written again, so autosaves only write new images. Image files that no paint uses any more are removed; other files under `images/` are never touched.
- **Renderer**: gradients in the node's own space, as Figma places them (the diamond gradient is a runtime shader). Images in each scale mode, decoded once after the recording that needs them. The layers that use an image are then re-recorded, and `RendererOptions.onImageReady` asks the host for a frame. `RendererOptions.images` takes a map or a lookup; the default is the document's own images, and `whenImagesReady()` is there for tests. Missing images draw as a faint grey. Paint opacity and blend modes; layer blend modes, with `NORMAL` frames isolating children that blend and `PASS_THROUGH` letting them blend with what is below. `LINEAR_BURN` and `LINEAR_DODGE` use runtime blenders. Drop and inner shadows with spread are cast from the outline of shapes and filled frames. Rectangles, rounded rectangles and ellipses use a blurred-box shader, with no offscreen mask. Groups, text and frames without a fill cast shadows from their pixels, through a layer image filter. Layer blur, background blur (a backdrop filter clipped to the outline) and stroke alignment (clipped, at double width). Text draws once per visible fill, gradients included. Hit-testing still ignores shadows; outside strokes on closed paths now reach their full weight.
- Bench: the scene adds a soft shadow on each of the 100 screens, 1,500 gradient fills, 400 small drop shadows and 400 inner shadows. Run it with `--plain` for the old scene, or with `--fx=…` to keep only some of these. 10,000 layers on Apple M1 (ANGLE Metal, 2× pixel density): 97.6–98.7% of frames within 16.7 ms, 6.5–7.0 ms draw per frame, recorded in 146–160 ms. With one edit per frame: 97.3%. The plain scene: 100%, 4.8 ms, 106 ms (before this change: 100%, 4.8 ms, 102 ms).
- Tests: 80 new ones (594 in all, plus 1 todo). Format (47): round trips, every validation refusal, image read and write with each warning, zip, SHA-256 against node:crypto, and 2 × 1,000 more fuzz cases with paints, effects and damaged images. Renderer (25): pixel tests for each paint type, effect, blend-mode family, stroke alignment and image scale mode, plus picture caching and hit-testing. Host-web (4): binary saves. Core (4): document images.
### Fixed (2026-10-18, QC round 6: performance and geometry)
- **Big pages stay smooth.** With 12,289 layers in one frame, dragging Hero went from p95 133 ms to 17 ms per frame, and resizing Desktop from 150 ms to 17 ms. The Layers panel now draws only the rows in view, and a drag step re-renders no rows. It is an ARIA tree with `aria-level`, `aria-setsize` and `aria-posinset`. Keyboard: ↑/↓, Home/End and PageUp/PageDown move the selection, Shift extends it, ←/→ go to the parent or first child, and Enter or Space selects. Shift-click and ⌘-click ranges still work, and a selection made elsewhere scrolls into view. A top-level frame with more than 256 children records them in chunks of about √n, so an edit re-records one chunk and replays the rest. A new e2e suite, `perf.mjs`, covers this (17 checks).
- **Resizing many layers takes one layout pass** (`resizeMany` in `@illigma/layout`, `resizeLayers` and `setBoxValues` in the app). For 1,000 children of an auto layout frame, typing W went from 555 ms to 7 ms and a live resize step from about 600 ms to 9 ms. For 4,000 children: 9.7 s to 19 ms.
- **Vectors scale with their node.** Whenever layout changes a vector's size (handles, W and H, Scale and Stretch constraints, Fill and Hug), its network's vertices and handles are rescaled. Corner radii keep their value, and an axis that was 0 long (a straight line) is left alone. One undo restores the exact network.
- Layout rounds the positions and sizes it computes to a millionth, so no more `40.010000000000005`. Values it leaves alone stay exactly as stored.
- Path data with handles too large to be numbers is refused with `FormatError`. Very large coordinates are written in full, never as `Infinity`. The prototype importer turns a path its scaling overflows into a "couldn't read the outline" warning instead of failing the import.
- Open subpaths are filled as in SVG when the path also has closed ones. A region can't close a subpath without a stroked segment, so such a path gets no region: every run fills as if closed, with the nonzero rule.
- Hover over huge vectors: curves are flattened to a 0.05 px tolerance, capped at 250,000 points per path. For a 100,000-segment vector, the first hover went from 399 ms and 6.4 million points to 60 ms and 201,000 points.
- Hit-testing includes square caps and miter joins (up to the miter limit of 4).
- Star, polygon, vector and line layers have their own icons in the Layers panel.

### Added (2026-10-18, export library)
- New package `@illigma/export` (MPL-2.0): PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF files from any layer or page, with Figma's export settings (format, scale or fixed width/height, suffix, `contentsOnly`, `useAbsoluteBounds`, SVG id attribute, JPG quality). `exportNode`, `exportNodes` (batch, with per-file failures that don't stop the rest), `exportPdf` (one page per layer) and `planExportFilenames`. It runs in the browser and in Node, and never writes to disk itself. No editor UI yet.
- File names follow Figma: `<layer name><suffix>.<ext>`, folders from `/` in names, made safe for every disk. In a batch, taken names get `-1`, `-2`… instead of replacing a file.
- PNG and JPG are drawn by the renderer's own code on an offscreen surface, so they match the canvas. Transparent PNG, JPG over white, whole-pixel sizes, and a plain-language error past 16,384 px a side or 134 million pixels. JPG falls back to a built-in baseline encoder, because CanvasKit's default build only encodes PNG.
- SVG is real vectors: groups, clip paths for frames, rect, ellipse and path elements, winding rules, strokes with caps and joins, opacity, rotation, and text as `<text>` wrapped where the canvas wraps it. The output is deterministic and escaped.
- PDF is written by a small built-in PDF 1.7 writer, as vector paths with transparency groups and clipping. Text is embedded as an image for now (it can't be selected yet).
- `@illigma/renderer` (additive): `PageRenderer.drawSubtree(canvas, id)` draws one layer and its children onto any canvas. `PageRenderer.layoutText(id)` returns a text layer's lines and baselines. The renderer reads documents through the `RenderSource` interface (a `Document` is one). `CLIPPING` is exported.
- Tests: 44 export tests (pixel checks after decoding, golden SVG and PDF output, well-formed XML, PDF cross-reference checks, file names, size guard, batch failures) and 2 renderer tests.
### Fixed (2026-10-18, editor behaviour QC round 6)
- Shift+A on absolute children of an auto layout frame no longer makes them vanish: they join the new frame's flow, and the new frame is absolute at their bounds when all of them were.
- Files are laid out once as they open (deep, every page, no undo step), so the first unrelated edit no longer makes layers jump and recovery replays onto the same layout.
- Constraints on a group set its children's (the card shows their common value or Mixed).
- The Fill and Hug cascades apply only along a frame's stack; across it, Fill and Hug live together.
- Auto gap on a frame hugging its stack makes that axis Fixed at its size, so the spacing stays visible.
- Removing auto layout from nested frames leaves no stale Fill.
- Arrow-key reordering of a non-contiguous selection steps each layer past its neighbour (A, C of ABCD down: BADC).
- Number fields: an arrow step followed at once by Tab is kept (one undo step); typed values stay in range (positions and sizes ±1,000,000, padding and gaps up to 100,000); an edit the document refuses shows a short alert and changes nothing, never an uncaught error.
- Hit tolerance follows zoom (3 screen pixels); Shift+A follows the character typed (AZERTY); on macOS only ⌘ skips constraints while resizing; dragging an auto layout child far outside its frame drops nothing; a `#/file/<id>` hash change opens that file.

### Added (2026-10-18, layout engine wired into editing)
- `@illigma/core`: `Document` takes a `normalize` hook (`DocumentOptions.normalize`, type `Normalizer`). It completes every edit inside the edit itself: at the end of each top-level `transact`, after each `LiveEdit.update` step, and after `replay`. Its operations join the same undo step, so one undo reverts an edit and what the hook added. Undo and redo never run it again; they restore exact values. If it throws, the edit is rolled back. `doc.normalizer` reads it back.
- The web app lays out every edit. Each document it creates or opens (`loadDocument`, `sampleDocument`, Keep's replay copy) uses `relayout` on the changed ids as its normalizer. Drag steps, Properties edits, ⌘D, Delete, drawing and recovered edits are all laid out (`apps/web/src/editor/layout.ts`).
- Canvas resize goes through layout `resize`: children follow their constraints. Hold ⌘/Ctrl to resize without constraints, as in Figma. Resizing a Hug or Fill layer by an edge switches that axis to Fixed (an auto layout frame's sizing mode too). Typing W or H in Properties does the same. X and Y of a layer in auto layout belong to its parent and change nothing.
- Dragging a layer in an auto layout flow reorders it instead of moving it. The selection box follows the pointer. A 2 px insertion line (`accent/default`) shows the slot, picked by pointer position along the flow. The order changes on release, as one undo step, and Escape cancels. Arrow keys along the flow reorder by one step. Absolute-positioned children still move freely. Pressing on a selected layer nested inside the one a click would pick (a card's child) keeps the selection.
- ⌘D inside auto layout puts the copy right after the original, and the frame reflows. Delete reflows too.
- The sample file now has an auto layout "Card" (vertical, padding 24, gap 16, three children). Avatar is pinned to the right edge, so resizing Desktop shows constraints at work.
- Tests: 10 for the core hook (undo/redo exact, nested, rollback, live steps and cancel, replay) and 17 for the wiring, including a 10,000-layer live drag and resize within a time budget. There are 14 new e2e checks in `canvas.mjs` (constraints, ⌘-resize, reorder by drag and Escape, ⌘D, edge resize, Delete, arrow reorder, undo). The smoke, recovery and save-errors counts are updated for the 7-layer sample.

### Added (2026-10-18, auto layout and constraints inspector)
- **Auto layout card** in Properties (board "01 Auto layout"): add (+) or remove (−); direction (Horizontal, Vertical, Wrap) as a radio group; a 9-point alignment grid mapped to `primaryAxisAlignItems` / `counterAxisAlignItems` by direction; gap as a number or **Auto** (space between, via the gap menu or by typing "auto"), plus a row gap when wrapping; padding as horizontal and vertical pairs or each side ("Individual padding"). Every control works on several frames at once and shows "Mixed" where they differ.
- **Sizing menus** on W and H (board "21 · width sizing menu"): Fixed, Hug contents and Fill container, offering only what applies (Hug for auto layout frames, Fill for layers in an auto layout flow). A Fill child makes a hugging parent Fixed, Hug on a frame makes its Fill children Fixed, and typing a size on a Hug or Fill axis makes it Fixed, as in Figma. **Absolute position** toggle for children of auto layout frames.
- **Constraints card** for layers in plain frames and absolute layers in auto layout: Left, Right, Left & right, Center, Scale and Top, Bottom, Top & bottom, Center, Scale, with the constraint widget (Shift-click a second edge for both). A typed frame width or height now moves children by their constraints.
- **Shift+A** adds auto layout to a selected plain frame (direction, gap, alignment and padding guessed from the children, the frame hugs them); with several layers, a shape or an auto layout frame selected, it wraps them in a new auto layout frame. ⌥⇧A removes auto layout. One undo step each.
- Inspector writes go through `setLayoutProps` (`apps/web/src/editor/layout-props.ts`), one `Document.transact` each; layout runs once in the document's normalizer, so the canvas reflows at once and one undo reverts the setting and everything it moved. The X/Y/W/H fields use `setBoxField` from `layout.ts`.
- Removing auto layout turns its children's Fill sizing to Fixed and a hugging frame to Fixed, as in Figma.
- Tests: 27 unit tests (alignment grid ↔ fields in both directions, Shift+A guesses, wrapping, sizing rules, constraints) and a new `autolayout.mjs` e2e (80 checks); the a11y e2e now runs axe on the auto layout card, the open sizing menu and the constraints card.

### Fixed (2026-10-18, prototype import QC round 5)
- `import:prototype --force` only replaces an Illigma file: a package folder whose manifest.json says `"format": "illigma"`, or a zip holding one. It never replaces another folder with a manifest.json (web projects, browser extensions) or any other file, never the folder it runs in or a parent of it, never the input or a folder holding it, and never writes through a symbolic link. The new file is written under a temporary name and renamed into place, so a failed import leaves the old one as it was.
- The command explains a folder as input, unreadable input, a file in the output's path and an unwritable output folder in plain language, naming the right path. Its logic moved to `src/import-cli.ts` with tests.
- Importer: input with a UTF-8 byte order mark is read; breaking a loop of frames only moves a frame of the loop (frames hanging below it keep their parent) and counts each loop once; missing text settings no longer count as broken values; large groups and older documents with many frames import in near-linear time.
### Fixed (2026-10-18, canvas QC round 5)
- Small layers move: inside the selection an edge grabs at most a quarter of the box, so the middle of a tiny layer always moves it; its corners stay reachable from outside and edge handles hide below 24 px. A line's own length has no top/bottom edge.
- Drags can't get stuck: a move with no button down ends the drag, window blur or a hidden tab cancels it, a second finger or pen is ignored, and right-click never opens the browser menu on the canvas. Closing the tab, going Home, Keep and app updates first keep an open drag instead of saving without it.
- Clicking a handle without dragging keeps the selection; Escape between press and drag drops the press. Handles and the box sit on the layers a resize changes (not locked layers, nor a selected parent's child). Resizing keeps an unscaled axis as it is (a line stays 0 px high).
- ⌘D is never passed to the browser (bookmark dialog), even from a field. Undo of Delete (and redo of a duplicate or drawn shape) selects those layers again. Layers panel: Shift-click selects a range, ⌘-click toggles.
- Recovery: edits made in the last 250 ms before the tab closes are stashed synchronously in localStorage on pagehide and merged into the journal on the next load (matched by session id and seq, so nothing is offered twice or after a save holds it).
- The 10,000-node layout budget test takes the best of 5 runs after a warm-up, so it no longer fails under parallel test load.

### Added (2026-10-18, Phase 1: vector networks, stars and polygons)
- Format v0, optional fields named after Figma's Plugin API (files without them save byte for byte as before): `vectorNetwork` on VECTOR nodes (`vertices` with optional `cornerRadius`, `segments` with Bézier `tangentStart`/`tangentEnd`, `regions` with `NONZERO`/`EVENODD` winding and closed `loops`, in the node's own space), `pointCount` (POLYGON 3, STAR 5 by default; 3 to 1,000) and `innerRadius` (STAR, 0–1, default 0.382), `strokeCap` (`NONE`, `ROUND`, `SQUARE`) and `strokeJoin` (`MITER`, `BEVEL`, `ROUND`). Strict validation: finite numbers, vertex and segment indices in range, every region loop a closed chain, at most 100,000 vertices and segments, 10,000 regions and 200,000 loop entries; JSON Schema entries.
- `@illigma/format`: `vectorNetworkFromSvgPath(d, { windingRule })` reads SVG path data (M L H V C S Q T A Z, absolute and relative, compact numbers and arc flags; quadratics raised to cubics, arcs split into cubics of at most 90°) and `svgPathFromVectorNetwork(net)` writes it; both refuse bad input with a `FormatError` only (fuzz-tested). Also `vectorNetworkProblem`, `walkVectorLoop`, `vectorStrokeChains`, `vectorNetworkPaths`, `arcToCubics`.
- Renderer: VECTOR draws its regions (each with its winding rule; a network with no regions fills its runs as if closed, as in Figma and SVG) and strokes every segment, joining through points shared by exactly two segments; STAR and POLYGON draw Figma's geometry (inscribed in the box's ellipse, first point at the top centre) with rounded corners from `cornerRadius`; LINE strokes along its width. Stroke caps and joins apply to every shape. A 0-weight stroke no longer draws a hairline. Pixel tests for stars, polygons, a curved even-odd ring, open paths and lines.
- Hit-testing: stars and polygons by their shape; vectors by their filled regions (holes excluded) and by their stroke; lines by their stroke. Strokes reach half their weight plus a tolerance (`hitTest(..., { tolerance })`, default 2 world units; `PageRenderer.hitTest` uses 3 screen pixels).
- Prototype import: path outlines (`d`, scaled from `baseW` × `baseH` to the layer's size) become vector networks with even-odd fill and the prototype's round caps and joins; stroke weights scale with the path. The "path outlines" warning is gone; unreadable outlines are kept as empty vectors with a warning.
- Benchmark: the 10,000-layer scene now mixes in stars, polygons and curved vectors with holes (1,300 of the 10,000 layers).
